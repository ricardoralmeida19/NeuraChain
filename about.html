About NeuraChain

Manufacturing intelligence, built by people who’ve run operations.

NeuraChain Solutions is an industrial operations technology and services company headquartered in Oakland, California, built by seasoned professionals with more than 25 years of combined global supply chain and advanced manufacturing experience across five countries on three continents.

We exist because small and mid-size manufacturers, utilities, and the infrastructure and data center builders adding capacity across the U.S. face the same operational challenges as the world's most advanced factories, but have rarely had access to the same quality of expertise. NeuraChain closes that gap with one validated method, defined service modules and a proprietary AI platform, starting with immediate, measurable results.

Because the method, deliverables and platform are standardized, the same results can be delivered in any plant, utility or capital project, in any state. Every engagement adds to a shared base of cost models and supplier intelligence that strengthens the next one.
